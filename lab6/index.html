Lab 6: Hierarchical GDP Treemaps

Description

This dataset organizes 27 countries into areas and continents. Rectangle area represents GDP in billions of U.S. dollars, while color represents whether GDP increased, was unchanged, or decreased.

Squarified treemap

GDP by continent, area, and country

Hover a country for its hierarchy, GDP, and status. Rectangle area is proportional to a country's GDP.

Slice-and-dice treemap

GDP by continent, area, and country

The same data and encodings use alternating horizontal and vertical subdivision.

Design description

In both treemaps, rectangle area encodes GDP because area supports direct part-to-whole comparison within the world, continents, and subregions. Green, gray, and red encodes categorical attributes for status (Increase, Unchanged, or Decrease). Continent and area headers, borders, and country labels make the nested hierarchy visible. Tooltips are provided for showing exact values. The squarified layout creates compact rectangles with similar aspect ratios, so country labels and comparisons among countries are generally easier to read. The slice-and-dice layout alternates the split direction at each hierarchy level. This makes parent-child membership clear but small-GDP countries can become too narrow with labels that do not fit.
