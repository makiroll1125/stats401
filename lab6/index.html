Lab 6: Hierarchical GDP Treemaps

Dataset description

This synthetic dataset organizes 27 countries into areas and continents. Rectangle area represents GDP in billions of U.S. dollars, while color represents whether GDP increased, was unchanged, or decreased.

Squarified treemap

GDP by continent, area, and country

Hover a country for its hierarchy, GDP, and status. Rectangle area is proportional to GDP.

Slice-and-dice treemap

GDP by continent, area, and country

The same data and encodings use alternating horizontal and vertical subdivision.

Design description

In both treemaps, rectangle area encodes GDP because area supports direct part-to-whole comparison within the world, continents, and subregions. A blue, gray, and orange palette encodes categorical attributes for status (Increase, Unchanged, and Decrease). Continent and area headers, borders, and country labels make the nested hierarchy visible, while tooltips provide exact values. The squarified layout creates compact rectangles with similar aspect ratios, so country labels and comparisons among countries are generally easier to read. The slice-and-dice layout alternates the split direction at each hierarchy level. Its aligned bands make parent-child membership clear but small-GDP countries can become too narrow with labels that do not fit.
