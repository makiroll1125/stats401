Design description

Node fill identifies sector, shape identifies region, and area shows current transaction volume. Thin dashed guides separate the three region zones, while inactive companies remain faint but present. Link color identifies transaction type, width shows amount, and opacity shows transaction count.

Temporal network questions

  1. How does overall network connectivity change across the 60 days?

    Connectivity seem to cycle between sparse and busy days. As time increases, active relationships appears to rise from 6-10 to 8-12.

  2. Which companies become more central or commercially active over time?

    Fusion Electronics, Keystone Materials, and Granite Wholesale appear to becomes more prominent over time. Delta and Ion Systems become much more noticeably active particularly near the end.

  3. Are there periods when the network consists of relatively separate clusters?

    Yes. It appears for the first ~20 days, most companies cluster into 3 or 4 separate, small groups. Around day 20, most daily relationships consist of two or three larger groups.

  4. Which commercial relationships appear, disappear, or become stronger over time?

    Early relationships repeatedly alternate by day. Fusion's links to Granite and Juniper appear in the middle period, followed by stronger Cedar-related and Ion-related relationships after day ~40.

  5. Does the network become more or less cross-regional over time?

    It appears to become more cross-regional with cross-region links increasing much more noticeably around day ~40.
