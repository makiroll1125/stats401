Design reflection

I extended Lab 7's animated temporal commercial network. Voice commands allow users to say Play, Pause, or Reset, invoking the same buttons as mouse input. Camera-based hand tracking using hand-landmark recognition translates horizontal hand movement into changes to the day slider: left moves the time backwards and right moves forward. These interactions support scanning changes in commercial relationships and stopping to inspect a particular day without continuously reaching for a mouse. Voice suits discrete playback actions while the hand movement provides a spatial mapping to time. Adding smoothing and a movement threshold to the hand-based tracking will reduce small tracking fluctuations, making the day-scroller less overly-sensitive. Some limitations include background speech overlaying user speech, low lighting or occlusion blocking out hand-tracking support, and browser support varying between devices.
