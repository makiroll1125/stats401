Lab 2: Multivariate Visualization with D3

This visualization explores the relationship among population, temperature, development level, and region in various cities.

Design Justification

Population size is represented by the x-axis position, allowing viewers to easily compare cities from smaller to larger populations. Development level is mapped to the y-axis, using the categorical levels Low, Medium, and High visually correlating with their location on the y-axis to show how cities differ in development level. Temperature is represented by color, using a blue-to-red gradient where cooler temperatures appear blue and warmer temperatures appear red. This allows viewers to easily notice relationships between temperature and other variables. Region is represented by the orientation of the triangle, with triangles pointing north, east, south, or west.

These mappings were chosen to make each variable visually distinct from each other and easier to interpret. I hope viewers can quickly identify relationships between temperature and development level and population size.
